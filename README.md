# Driving ARPU Growth Through Customer Usage Behavior and Plan Optimization: A Telco Analytics Case Study

## 📌 Company Overview
TelcoX is a growing telecommunications startup that has been operating for approximately two years, offering mobile data and voice subscription plans to individual customers across multiple regions.

TelcoX is a growing telecommunications startup that has been operating for approximately two years, offering mobile data and voice subscription plans to individual customers across multiple regions.

By the end of 2025, the company was facing significant competition from other providers and was keen to implement improvements as a matter of urgency. Meanwhile, the internal team had also conducted an evaluation of the increasingly stagnant Average Revenue Per User (ARPU), and had identified plans which did not meet the standards used by customers.

To address this challenge, TelcoX aims to **leverage customer usage behavior analytics** to **improve plan alignment**, **enhance customer value**, and **drive sustainable ARPU growth through data-driven decision making**.

## 🎯 Problem Statement
**“How can TelcoX improve ARPU by aligning subscription plan with customer usage behavior?"**

## 🧩 Objective
* Understand customer usage behavior across different subs
* Identify mismatch plan, including overusage and underusage segment
* Evaluate the impact of usage behavior on ARPU and Add-on Purhcases
* Provide data-driven recomendations to optimized plan allocation and revenue growth

## 🔑 Key Insights
* A significant portion of high-usage customers remain on lower-tier plans, indicating unrealized ARPU potential.
* Medium usage customers show strong add-on adoption, suggesting readiness for plan upgrades or bundled offerings.
* Light usage customers are frequently over-allocated in higher quota plans, creating cost inefficiency without revenue upside.
* Add-on purchases act as a revenue bridge, signaling unmet needs in existing subscription plans.

## 👨‍💻 Business Recommendation

**1. Upsell High-Usages Customer**

Prioritize proactive upgrades to Premium or Unlimited plans for customers consistently exceeding quota.

**2. Convert Medium Users via Bundled Offers**

Introduce data + add-on bundles to transition medium users toward higher-value plans.

**3. Optimizes Plans for Lights Users**

Encourage right-sizing through standard plans to improve perceived value and reduce network inefficiency.

**4. Leverage Add-On Behavior as Upsell Signals**

Use frequent add-on purchases as indicators for plan mismatch and upgrade readiness.

**5. Deploy Usage-Based Nudges**

Implement data usage notifications to guide customers toward optimal plans before overusage occurs.

## 📈 Potential Impact
* If 20% of misaligned heavy users upgrade to higher-tier plans, TelcoX could unlock incremental ARPU growth.
* Upselling medium users through bundled plans may increase add-on conversion while reducing overusage friction.
* Plan optimization for light users can improve customer satisfaction and reduce unnecessary network cost.
